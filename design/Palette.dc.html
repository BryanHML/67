STRUCTURE
Borders are 2px Ink everywhere — one weight, no exceptions.
Shadows are hard offsets, never blurred: 2px on tiles and chips, 3px buttons, 6px panels. On phones, rows carry none.
Radii: 999px pills, 14px rows and tiles, 22px panels.
Touch targets never below 44px; phone rows are 60px.
Yellow marks a gesture you hold. Coral marks one you move. That is the only thing colour encodes.
